        FAQ
        
Questions patients often ask

        
Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp and the clinic team will help.

        Ask on WhatsApp
      

      

        
Can I continue my current medicines?
Yes. Please continue any treatment your doctor has prescribed. Bring a list of your medicines to the first visit so the doctor has the full picture.

        
Should both partners come for the first visit?
It helps. Infertility can involve either partner, and understanding both histories makes the treatment plan more complete.

        
How long does treatment take?
It differs from person to person. The doctor will discuss a review schedule at your first consultation and adjust it as you progress.

        
Can I consult online?
Yes. Online consultations are available by video or phone. Book on 96335 44660.

        
Can I take homoeopathic treatment during IVF or IUI?
Talk to both your fertility specialist and Dr. Seena first. Homoeopathic care is given alongside, not in place of, your specialist's advice.
